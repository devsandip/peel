# Synkies checkpoint and brand decision

2026-10-28 16:53 IST. First journal entry. I reconstructed this checkpoint from the public README, canonical PRD/SPEC, WORKLOG, Git history through PR105 and the current issue106 branch. This is a present-day account, not a backdated series.

## What happened

I started this app as Peel: the floating capture experience of Mac Stickies with an organized, searchable library across devices. I settled on two library-wide modes. Free On-device keeps note content on devices and periodically syncs paired devices remotely. Paid Cloud stores the complete library in DynamoDB and promises live delivery. Google Drive is out of scope.

I have a local Mac development app and a browser development client. Notes save locally, the library supports organization and word search, and independent Mac note windows use one green minus to save and put the note away. Component tests and mockups are not proof that every native interaction or performance target has passed. The canonical v0.7 mockups cover110 screens plus five dark exports.

I moved development into the clean public devsandip/peel repository. Earlier history remains in the private archive. I use small single-purpose PRs, merge each ready PR after checks, and track current deliverables in [Project12](https://github.com/users/devsandip/projects/12). I do not hold ready work for a sprint boundary.

Cloud work has reached reviewed local foundations: service/protocol experiments, infrastructure/guard preparation, a durable funding ledger, original recipe/context persistence and pure stack-create identity descriptors. PR105 records the next recoverable creation-journal design. It does not implement that journal or authorize another AWS run. The managed-service smoke and confirmed independent teardown are still missing.

Today I paused [issue106](https://github.com/devsandip/peel/issues/106) at Sandip's request. Its plan, compatibility tests and SQLite mechanics extraction are committed locally on codex/cloud-owned-sqlite-engine. The same25 actual Node24 namespace/ledger tests pass before and after extraction, including real process interruption cases, and TypeScript passes. Focused final review, broader checks and hosted gates are still pending. It has not been pushed or merged.

Sandip renamed the app **Synkies** and selected **Folded Horizon**, generated by Antigravity, from its v2 package. I inspected that source and its light lockup. The name and asset selection are decided; shipping surfaces and mockups still need the following branding PRs. I will resume issue106 after that change.

## Where we are now

The local development app is usable for isolated testing. It is not the complete free or paid public product. Remote peer sync, enabled paid/live Cloud, iPhone, local meaning-search acceptance, native release/performance gates and public distribution remain. No public beta, production service or real billing is enabled.

[M2.2](https://github.com/devsandip/peel/milestone/2) stays open. Audit51, managed31 and native72 retain their explicit blockers. Expired AWS allowances and leases do not become valid because build work continues. The stopped relay, audit, native-control and peer/WebKit probes remain stopped. Unknown local drafts, private note data and the private archive remain preserved.

## What I believe

Fast local capture is the foundation. Remote syncing and Cloud need their own end-to-end evidence before I call them built. A durable local record or successful test fixture does not prove provider ownership or cleanup. The new brand should change what people see while preserving their existing storage and identities.
